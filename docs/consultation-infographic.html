Tongue Detection deterministic

Roboflow serverless workflow: detect and crop, server-side. Confidence gate stays in app code. This step — not the Advisor — is the sole authority on whether a photo shows a tongue.

Tongue Photo persisted deterministic

Every returned crop is kept, gate-passed or not. A standalone research dataset, outside Consultation memory: never written to the Health Profile, never affected by the Relevance Gate.

Advisor Model agentic
model slot — currently Gemini 2.5 Flash

Retrieves grounding passages, then produces the Tongue Assessment and replies in Thai.

Why “Assessment”, and never a clinical claim

The system produces TTM-grounded interpretations for self-care. It makes no clinical determination of disease, and defers to a physician for anything clinically serious. The wording is enforced in the project’s own ubiquitous language, not only in the user interface.

TTM Corpus deterministic

Thai sources only — one Chroma collection, BGE-M3 multilingual embeddings, paragraph-level chunks carrying book / printed page / paragraph provenance.

Then vs now — the same finding, re-observed

Not a clinical determination

A TTM self-care advisor. It produces Assessments — TTM-grounded interpretations for self-care — never a clinical determination of disease, and it tells users to see a doctor for anything clinically serious.

Red-flag escalation

When a red flag appears, the Advisor escalates to “see a doctor” and the Topic Menu is suppressed — the system never offers a browsing menu on top of an escalation.

Photos kept for research

Users are told at first contact that tongue photos are kept. Every crop is retained, including ones the confidence gate rejected; rejected crops are never described, never assessed, and never shown back.

★ ธาตุเจ้าเรือน is derived deterministically from birth date in code — never set by a model. The month-to-element table is being confirmed against the corpus.
